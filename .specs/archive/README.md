# Archived features

Harness features of F0 whose mechanisms ADR 029 (lean harness, 2026-11-10) removed: the retry ledger, the merge
guard and verdict hook, the gate's read-only and polling rules, the proof preflight and the trailer-based phase state.
Their specs, designs and tasks stay here as the record of what was built and why it was removed. Nothing in this
folder is checked or run.
