## Decisions to confirm

From `decisions.md → To confirm` (full text there). The ones that change a user-facing commitment or a sensor come first:

1. **Presence reads the game name in the Party panel**, not the automap. FR-27's PRD text in Claude Docs needs the edit.
2. **Ladder and era are declared by the player** on the site. FR-02's PRD text needs the edit.
3. **English first** for the agent reading, Portuguese as a later language pack. Exit criterion 4 stays Partial until
   then.
4. **The encrypted zip of your five screenshots** in `apps/agent/ocr-lab/`: an exception to "never commit a game
   screenshot", guarded by `check-encrypted-zip.mjs`. Real screenshots are the OCR proof, and the drawn-fixture OCR
   tests are informational.
5. **`window_capture.rs` joins the agent's `unsafe` allow-list** (a sensor change).
6. **The `global-hotkey` crate** for the ready-check hotkey. `tauri-plugin-global-shortcut` stays banned.
7. **Google Fonts** on the site: every visitor's IP reaches Google. The option is to self-host the fonts (LGPD).
8. **AWS-21 now expects "Formed"** after a reload: its requirement changed with LBR-02.
9. Smaller: the OAuth `fetch` adapter instead of `openid-client`, the routes without `returnTo`, the site labels for
   eras and pt-BR difficulties, an inconsistent card title is not sent, six readings per region with agreement, names
   compared without case, `Ø`→`0` in the Party match, the QUE-05 test control, `ClockScheduler.every` on the injected
   clock, no demo mode on previews.

New from this review:

10. **The load cost ceiling.** `load.bytes_per_player_hour` is 421,400 on the F1 journey. The ceiling is 125,000,
    and the F0 baseline of 113,827 measured a room-only journey. The F1 journey streams character, queue, ready-check
    and room events. Against Render's 5 GB/month egress (ADR 005) that is about 12,000 player-hours a month. Options:
    (a) raise the ceiling to the F1 measurement (your call, rule 4); (b) keep it and make cutting stream bytes an F2
    task; (c) both: raise it for F1 and add the reduction to F2. Recommended: (c).
11. **Workers on Sonnet by default** (H-001, ADR 025): about 1–4 rounds against 3–9, and no stalls. Needs an ADR
    superseding 025's worker model.
