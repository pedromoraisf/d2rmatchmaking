# ADR 007 — Server-Sent Events for push

**Status**: Accepted
**Date**: 2026-10-30
**Deciders**: maintainer

## Context

Clients need room and queue updates in under 2 s (NFR-01); traffic is server-to-client.

## Decision

One SSE stream per client at `GET /v1/stream` with `Last-Event-ID` resume and a 25 s keep-alive. Clients send commands over REST.

## Alternatives considered

WebSocket; polling.

## Consequences

Simpler behind proxies and the Pages Function; cookie-authenticated `EventSource`. Validate in F0 that SSE crosses the Pages Function without cuts.
